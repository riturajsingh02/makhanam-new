WHO WE ARE

THE MAKHANAM STORY

Connecting the origin of makhana with modern business.

Makhanam is a premium raw makhana processing and wholesale supply company rooted in Bihar.

We focus on bringing carefully processed and graded raw makhana to businesses that require dependable quality and bulk supply. Our approach combines the heritage of Bihar's makhana ecosystem with a professional, business-focused supply process.

From sourcing and processing to grading and wholesale supply, every stage is approached with attention to consistency and the requirements of our partners.
